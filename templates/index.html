Why it matters

More medicines, more chances of interaction

When someone takes five or more medicines at once, it's called polypharmacy. It's common in older adults with several health conditions — and every extra medicine is another chance that two of them clash.

Many of these problems are easy to prevent once a doctor or pharmacist knows about them. MediGuard helps you spot them early so you know what to ask.

What MediGuard looks for

  • 🔴 Serious pairs that can cause bleeding, kidney or heart problems
  • 🟡 Moderate combinations that need monitoring
  • 🔄 Two medicines of the same type taken together
  • 👵 Extra care points for older patients
